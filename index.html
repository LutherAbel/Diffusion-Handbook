Current state

  • RF, respaced DDPM, and deterministic DDIM were evaluated on controlled 2D Gaussian-mixture targets with matched NFE budgets.
  • The anisotropy, mode-separation, and finite-training-data sweeps each include three training seeds or independent finite datasets per size.
  • For mode radii 1.5 and 6.0, RF has the lowest mean SWD at every tested NFE. The earlier radius-6 single-seed DDPM crossover did not persist after aggregation.
  • The completed four-size finite-data sweep does not show a monotone size effect across all samplers. Oracle-field controls remain open.

Finite-data replication

Four training sizes, 500, 1,000, 2,500, and 5,000, were each evaluated with three independently drawn finite datasets. RF has the lowest mean at NFE 1 for all sizes. At NFE 5 through 100, the observed lowest mean changes with training size, and several gaps overlap their three-seed standard deviations. This is evidence against reading the earlier two-point, one-seed comparison as a general size trend.

Open the finite-data replication figure. Raw rows and aggregate values are in results/tables/sample_size_replication_raw.csv and results/tables/sample_size_replication.csv.

Interpretation boundary

For the recorded isotropic one-seed run, RF is lowest at all listed NFE values. In the three-seed mode-separation replication, RF is also lowest in mean SWD at both tested radii and all four NFE values. This supports a narrow result for these 2D targets, training budgets, seeds, and SWD protocol. It does not establish a general ordering of RF, DDPM, and DDIM.

DDPM and DDIM are equal at NFE 1 in this protocol because both use the same terminal prediction and the final DDPM jump adds no noise. Shared SWD directions are required for that equality to be measured fairly.

解讀邊界

在 recorded isotropic run 中，RF 在所有列出的 NFE 最低。在 recorded ellipse run 中，RF 在 NFE 1、5、20 最低，DDPM 在 NFE 100 最低。這只支持觀察到的 ranking 改變，不能推出 anisotropy 一般會讓 DDPM 較好。

本 protocol 下 DDPM 與 DDIM 在 NFE 1 相同，因為兩者使用相同 terminal prediction，DDPM 的最後 jump 不加 noise。要公平量到這個相等，必須共享 SWD directions。
