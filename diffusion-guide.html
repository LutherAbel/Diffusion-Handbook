Diffusion.py Guide

The 14 primary functions and classes in src/diffusion.py, one at a time. Shared variables are explained once in one table, and each entry then has three parts, what it does, its unique variables, and what it connects to.
TasksDiffusion.py guide

The core idea splits into two sentences. During training, we take clean data, add noise whose exact value we know, and teach a model to recover that noise. During sampling, we start from pure random noise, ask the trained model to estimate the noise inside, remove part of it, and repeat until clean data remains.

How the pieces connect

The diagram is easier to read one arrow at a time.

Arrow 1, a clean sample x0 comes from the GMM (Gaussian mixture model, a distribution built by mixing several Gaussian bumps). make_diffusion_batch adds a known amount of noise to it, producing xt.

Arrow 2, xt and the time t go into NoisePredictorMLP.

Arrow 3, the model outputs eps_hat, its guess of the noise that was added.

Arrow 4 happens during sampling only. The guessed noise is used to estimate the clean sample x0_hat, and a DDIM or DDPM update moves xt one step toward clean data. The loop repeats until the timestep reaches 0.

Schedule

make_diffusion_schedule(num_steps, beta_start, beta_end)

What it does. Training needs a lookup table answering "how much noise belongs at step t", and this function does exactly one thing, computing that table in advance.

It outputs three sequences. The first is betas, the fraction of new noise injected at each step.

The second is alphas, defined as 1 minus betas, which is the fraction of signal kept at each step.

The third is alpha_bars, the running product of alphas from step 1 up to step t. It answers "after t steps, how much of the original data is still left". Every sampler looks this one up.

Connects to. Its alpha_bars output is the shared table everyone reads, since make_diffusion_batch uses it to decide how much noise to add and all four samplers read it at every reverse step.

make_sampling_timesteps(num_diffusion_steps, nfe)

What it does. Sampling does not have to walk through all T steps. NFE (number of function evaluations, meaning how many times the model gets called) sets how many steps we actually take.

The function picks nfe+1 timestep indices, evenly spaced from T down to 0, in decreasing order. Consecutive pairs in that list become the (t, s) jumps every sampler loops over.

Models

NoisePredictorMLP(data_dim, hidden_dim=128)

What it does. This is the trainable network. It is an MLP (multi-layer perceptron, plain fully-connected layers and nothing fancier).

The input is the noisy sample x and the time t_model concatenated into one vector, and the output is a guess of the noise hiding inside x, with the same dimension as the data.

OracleNoiseModel(means, alpha_bars, sigma0) (new, task 011)

What it does. This model is not trained. It computes the mathematically correct noise prediction directly from the known GMM formula.

The reason it exists is that a normal experiment mixes two error sources, the model learning imperfectly and the sampler itself losing accuracy. Swapping in the oracle sets the first one to exactly zero, so whatever error remains belongs to the sampler alone.

The forward pass goes step by step. Step 1, convert the 0-to-1 time t_model back to an integer step t, and look up abar in the table.

Step 2, compute s2, the variance each GMM component has at step t. It combines the component's own spread (shrunk by abar) with the injected noise.

Step 3, compute centers, which is where each component's mean sits at step t (the original means shrunk by √abar).

Step 4, for every sample and every component, compute the squared distance sqr_dist, turn it into log_density, and apply softmax. The result corresponding_prob is "the probability this sample came from component k".

Step 5, a single Gaussian's score (the gradient of log density) is (center − x) / variance. For a mixture, average those per-component scores using the probabilities from step 4, which gives score.

Step 6, convert score to a noise prediction with eps_hat = −√(1−abar) · score, and return it.

Training

make_diffusion_batch(batch_size, cfg, means, alpha_bars)

What it does. Builds one batch of training examples, in four moves.

Move 1, draw clean samples x0 from the GMM.

Move 2, for each sample, pick a random timestep t_indices between 1 and T.

Move 3, draw standard Gaussian noise.

Move 4, mix them with the forward formula x_t = √ᾱ_t·x0 + √(1−ᾱ_t)·noise.

One key point is that noise is returned along with x_t, because the drawn noise is the correct answer the model will be graded against.

Unique variables.

VariableHoldsType
t_indicesRandom integer timesteps, one per sampletensor (n,)
alpha_bar_tᾱ looked up at each sample's own timesteptensor (n, 1)

Connects to. Called every step inside train_diffusion_model.

train_diffusion_model(cfg, seed, alpha_bars, …)

What it does. The standard training loop. Each iteration gets a fresh batch from make_diffusion_batch, lets the model predict the noise, measures the mean squared difference between prediction and true noise, and lets Adam (the optimizer) update the weights.

Because every batch draws brand-new GMM samples, this loop effectively trains on unlimited data.

Sampling

ddpm_jump_posterior(x_t, x0_hat, t, s, alpha_bars)

What it does. A helper used inside sampler loops. Given the current noisy sample x_t and the current estimate of the clean sample x0_hat, it answers the question of what distribution x_s should follow if we jump from step t to step s.

The answer is a Gaussian, so the function returns two things, the mean and the variance.

Unique variables.

VariableHoldsType
alpha_jumpᾱ_t / ᾱ_s, the fraction of signal this particular jump keepsscalar tensor
meanPosterior mean, a weighted mix of x0_hat and x_ttensor (n, dim)
variancePosterior variance σ̃²scalar tensor

Connects to. Called by sample_ddpm, sample_eta, and sample_sigma_hat.

sample_ddim(model, n_samples, cfg, nfe, alpha_bars, …)

What it does. The deterministic sampler. Deterministic means that no fresh randomness is added during the loop, so the same starting noise always produces the same output.

Each loop iteration does three things. First, the model predicts the noise in the current x.

Second, that prediction is used to back out an estimate of the clean sample, x0_hat = (x − √(1−ᾱ_t)·eps_hat) / √ᾱ_t.

Third, the DDIM update rebuilds x at the next step s, x = √ᾱ_s·x0_hat + √(1−ᾱ_s)·eps_hat. It reuses the predicted noise instead of drawing new noise, and that is exactly what makes it deterministic.

Unique variables.

VariableHoldsType
initial_noiseOptional fixed starting noise; if None, drawn freshtensor or None

Connects to. Produces generated samples for evaluation; the η=0 endpoint of the eta sweep.

sample_eta(model, n_samples, cfg, nfe, alpha_bars, eta, …)

What it does. A sampler with a dial. The dial is eta, a number from 0 to 1 controlling how much fresh noise gets injected at each reverse step.

At eta = 0 it reproduces DDIM (no fresh noise). At eta = 1 it matches the DDPM jump posterior (full fresh noise). Values in between interpolate.

Concretely, each step sets sigma = eta · √(posterior variance), then combines three ingredients, the clean estimate x0_hat, the predicted noise (weighted by noise_coef), and brand-new random noise step_noise (weighted by sigma).

There are two implementation details I want to mention. The clamp inside noise_coef only protects the square root from tiny negative rounding errors.

The other one is that step_noise is drawn every step even when eta = 0 makes it unused. The reason is that all eta settings then consume the same random number stream, so differences between eta values come from eta alone and not from different random draws. This technique is called common random numbers.

sample_sigma_hat(model, n_samples, cfg, nfe, alpha_bars, …)

What it does. The loop has the same shape as DDPM. It predicts noise, estimates x0_hat, and takes the posterior mean from ddpm_jump_posterior.

The one difference is the variance. Instead of the posterior variance, it uses the larger choice σ̂² = 1 − alpha_jump (the σ̂ row of Table 1 in Song et al. 2021).

The reason to keep it is that the paper reports this choice degrades badly when the step count is truncated (small NFE), so it serves as a control in our comparisons.

Unique variables. Only alpha_jump = ᾱ_t/ᾱ_s reused as the noise scale, √(1−alpha_jump)·step_noise. Everything else is in the shared table.

Connects to. Uses ddpm_jump_posterior for the mean; an ancestral-sampling control.

sample_ddpm(model, n_samples, cfg, nfe, alpha_bars, …)

What it does. Ancestral sampling, meaning that each reverse step draws an actual random sample from the jump posterior rather than following a deterministic rule.

Each loop iteration predicts noise, backs out x0_hat, asks ddpm_jump_posterior for mean and variance, then draws x_s = mean + √variance · step_noise.

At the final jump (s = 0) no noise is added, and the sampler returns the mean directly.

Unique variables. None, since everything it uses is in the shared table.

Connects to. Uses ddpm_jump_posterior; equivalent to sample_eta at eta = 1 (up to random-stream details); produces samples for evaluation.

任務Diffusion.py 教學

核心概念可以拆成兩句來講。訓練的時候，拿乾淨資料，加上我們知道確切數值的 noise，教 model 把那份 noise 猜回來。sampling 的時候，從純 random noise 出發，請訓練好的 model 估計裡面的 noise，去掉一部分，重複到只剩乾淨資料。

這些東西怎麼連起來

這張圖建議一次讀一個箭頭。

箭頭 1，乾淨樣本 x0 來自 GMM (Gaussian mixture model，由好幾個高斯分布加權組成的分布)。make_diffusion_batch 對它加入數值已知的 noise，得到 xt。

箭頭 2，xt 和時間 t 一起送進 NoisePredictorMLP。

箭頭 3，model 輸出 eps_hat，也就是它對「剛剛加了什麼 noise」的猜測。

箭頭 4 只在 sampling 時發生。用猜到的 noise 反推乾淨樣本的估計值 x0_hat，再由 DDIM 或 DDPM update 把 xt 往乾淨資料推進一步，重複到時間步走到 0。

Schedule

make_diffusion_schedule(num_steps, beta_start, beta_end)

它在幹嘛。Diffusion 的訓練需要「在第 t 步該加多少 noise」的一張對照表。這個函式的工作就只有一件事，把這張表事先算好。

它輸出三個數列。第一個是 betas，代表每一步「新加入的 noise 比例」。

第二個是 alphas，定義是 1 減掉 betas。意思是每一步「原訊號保留下來的比例」。

第三個是 alpha_bars，是 alphas 從第 1 步累乘到第 t 步的結果。意思是「經過 t 步之後，原始資料還剩下多少比例」。之後所有 sampler 查表查的主要就是它。

獨有變數。

變數名裝什麼型態
num_steps總共有幾步 (T)int
beta_start第 1 步的 noise 比例float
beta_end最後一步的 noise 比例float
betas每步新加的 noise 比例，第 0 格是 0tensor，長度 T+1
alphas每步保留原訊號的比例，= 1 − betastensor，長度 T+1

它連到誰。它的輸出 alpha_bars 就是大家共用的那張表，make_diffusion_batch 用它決定加多少 noise，四個 sampler 每一步 reverse update 也都查它。

make_sampling_timesteps(num_diffusion_steps, nfe)

它在幹嘛。sampling 不一定要走完全部 T 步。NFE (number of function evaluations，指 model 總共被呼叫幾次) 決定實際要走幾步。

這個函式從 T 到 0 均勻取出 nfe+1 個時間步索引，由大排到小。清單裡相鄰的兩格，就是之後每個 sampler 迴圈裡的一次 (t, s) 跳躍。

它連到誰。sample_ddim、sample_eta、sample_sigma_hat、sample_ddpm 四個 sampler 開頭都先呼叫它。

Models

NoisePredictorMLP(data_dim, hidden_dim=128)

它在幹嘛。這是可訓練的網路本體。它是一個 MLP (multi-layer perceptron，就是純粹的全連接層堆疊，沒有更花俏的結構)。

輸入是 noisy 樣本 x 和時間 t_model，兩個接成一條向量。輸出是「藏在 x 裡的 noise」的猜測值，維度跟資料一樣。

OracleNoiseModel(means, alpha_bars, sigma0) (task 011 新增)

它在幹嘛。這個 model 不用訓練。它直接用已知的 GMM 公式，算出數學上正確的 noise 預測。

它存在的原因是，一般實驗裡有兩個誤差來源混在一起，「model 學得不準」和「sampler 本身走一步掉一點精度」。把 oracle 換進去，第一個誤差來源就被精確歸零，剩下的誤差全都屬於 sampler。

forward 的計算可以一步一步拆開來看。第 1 步，把 0 到 1 的 t_model 換算回整數步 t，查表得到 abar。

第 2 步，算 s2，也就是第 t 步時每個 GMM component 的 variance。它由兩部分組成，component 自己的散布 (被 abar 縮小) 加上已注入的 noise。

第 3 步，算 centers，各 component 的中心在第 t 步的位置 (原本的 means 乘上 √abar 縮小)。

第 4 步，對每個樣本、每個 component，算距離平方 sqr_dist，轉成 log_density，再過 softmax。結果 corresponding_prob 的意思是「這個樣本來自第 k 個 component 的機率」。

第 5 步，單一高斯的 score (log density 的梯度) 是 (中心 − x) / variance。混合分布的 score，就是把各 component 的 score 用第 4 步的機率加權平均，得到 score。

第 6 步，用 eps_hat = −√(1−abar)·score 把 score 換成 noise 預測，回傳。

變數名裝什麼型態

s2第 t 步各 component 的 variancescalar tensor
centers縮到第 t 步的 component 中心tensor (K, dim)
diffx 減去每個中心tensor (n, K, dim)
sqr_dist到每個中心的距離平方tensor (n, K)
log_density差一個常數的 log densitytensor (n, K)

它連到誰。可以直接放進任何 sampler 取代 NoisePredictorMLP。experiments/eta_sweep_oracle.py 就是這樣用它。

Training

make_diffusion_batch(batch_size, cfg, means, alpha_bars)

它在幹嘛。造一批訓練資料，分四個動作。

動作 1，從 GMM 抽乾淨樣本 x0。

動作 2，每個樣本各自隨機抽一個 1 到 T 之間的時間步 t_indices。

動作 3，抽標準常態的 noise。

動作 4，用 forward 公式混合，x_t = √ᾱ_t·x0 + √(1−ᾱ_t)·noise。

有一個重點，noise 會跟 x_t 一起回傳，因為剛抽出來的 noise 本身就是訓練時要對答案的「正確答案」。

獨有變數。

變數名裝什麼型態
t_indices每個樣本抽到的整數時間步tensor (n,)
alpha_bar_t按各樣本自己的時間步查到的 ᾱtensor (n, 1)

它連到誰。train_diffusion_model 每一步都呼叫它。

train_diffusion_model(cfg, seed, alpha_bars, …)

它在幹嘛。標準的 training loop。每一輪做四件事，跟 make_diffusion_batch 拿一批新資料、讓 model 預測 noise、算預測值和真 noise 的均方差當 loss、讓 Adam (optimizer 的一種) 更新權重。

因為每一批都是重新抽的 GMM 樣本，這個 loop 等於是在無限資料上訓練。

獨有變數。

變數名裝什麼型態
training_stepsoptimizer 更新幾次int，預設 3000
learning_rateAdam 的步伐大小float，預設 2e-3
hidden_dim傳給 MLP 的寬度int，預設 128
loss與真 noise 的均方差scalar tensor
loss_history每一步的 loss 數值list of float

它連到誰。回傳 (model, means, loss_history)，訓練好的 model 就是每個 sampler 收到的那個。

Sampling

ddpm_jump_posterior(x_t, x0_hat, t, s, alpha_bars)

它在幹嘛。sampler 迴圈裡的小幫手。給它現在的 noisy 樣本 x_t 和目前估出的乾淨樣本 x0_hat，它回答的問題是，如果要從第 t 步跳到第 s 步，x_s 應該長什麼分布。

答案是一個高斯分布，所以它回傳兩個東西，mean 和 variance。

獨有變數。

變數名裝什麼型態
alpha_jumpᾱ_t / ᾱ_s，這一跳保留的訊號比例scalar tensor
meanposterior 的 mean，由 x0_hat 和 x_t 加權組成tensor (n, dim)
varianceposterior 的 variance σ̃²scalar tensor

它連到誰。sample_ddpm、sample_eta、sample_sigma_hat 都呼叫它。

sample_ddim(model, n_samples, cfg, nfe, alpha_bars, …)

它在幹嘛。deterministic 的 sampler。deterministic 的意思是，迴圈途中完全不再抽新的亂數，所以同一個起始 noise 永遠得到同一個輸出。

迴圈的每一輪做三件事。第一，model 預測目前 x 裡的 noise。

第二，用這個預測反推乾淨樣本的估計，x0_hat = (x − √(1−ᾱ_t)·eps_hat) / √ᾱ_t。

第三，用 DDIM 公式把 x 重組到下一步 s，x = √ᾱ_s·x0_hat + √(1−ᾱ_s)·eps_hat。它重複使用「預測出來的」noise 而不是抽新的，這正是它 deterministic 的原因。

獨有變數。

變數名裝什麼型態
initial_noise可選的固定起始 noise，None 就現抽tensor 或 None

它連到誰。產生用於 evaluation 的樣本，也是 eta sweep 的 η=0 端點。

sample_eta(model, n_samples, cfg, nfe, alpha_bars, eta, …)

它在幹嘛。一個有旋鈕的 sampler。旋鈕是 eta，一個 0 到 1 的數字，控制每一步 reverse update 要加入多少「新抽的」noise。

eta = 0 時它等於 DDIM (完全不加新 noise)。eta = 1 時它等於 DDPM 的 jump posterior (加足量新 noise)。中間的值就是兩者之間的內插。

具體來說，每一步先算 sigma = eta · √(posterior variance)，然後把三個成分加起來，分別是乾淨估計 x0_hat、預測 noise (權重是 noise_coef)、新抽的 step_noise (權重是 sigma)。

有兩個實作細節想提一下。noise_coef 裡的 clamp 只是保護開根號不要吃到浮點誤差造成的微小負數。

另一個是 step_noise 每一步都會抽，就算 eta = 0 用不到也照抽。原因是這樣一來，所有 eta 設定消耗的是同一串亂數，不同 eta 之間的差異就只來自 eta 本身，不會混入「抽到不同亂數」的差異。這個技巧叫 common random numbers。

獨有變數。

變數名裝什麼型態
eta0 到 1 的新 noise 旋鈕float
generatorstep noise 專用的亂數流torch.Generator
sigmaeta · √posterior_variance，新 noise 的大小scalar tensor
noise_coef√(1 − ᾱ_s − σ²)，預測 noise 的權重scalar tensor

它連到誰。呼叫 ddpm_jump_posterior；task 010/011 的 eta sweep 背後就是它。

sample_sigma_hat(model, n_samples, cfg, nfe, alpha_bars, …)

它在幹嘛。迴圈形狀跟 DDPM 一樣，先預測 noise、估 x0_hat，再跟 ddpm_jump_posterior 拿 posterior 的 mean。

唯一的差別在 variance。它不用 posterior variance，改用較大的選擇 σ̂² = 1 − alpha_jump (Song et al. 2021 的 Table 1 裡 σ̂ 那一列)。

留著它的原因是，論文回報這個選擇在步數被截短 (NFE 小) 的時候會嚴重劣化，所以它在我們的比較裡是一個對照組。

獨有變數。只有再利用 alpha_jump = ᾱ_t/ᾱ_s 當 noise 的大小，√(1−alpha_jump)·step_noise。其餘全在共用表。

它連到誰。mean 來自 ddpm_jump_posterior；作為 ancestral sampling 的對照組。

sample_ddpm(model, n_samples, cfg, nfe, alpha_bars, …)

它在幹嘛。ancestral sampling，意思是每一步 reverse 都真的從 jump posterior「抽」一個樣本出來，而不是走 deterministic 的規則。

迴圈的每一輪會預測 noise、反推 x0_hat、跟 ddpm_jump_posterior 拿 mean 和 variance，然後抽 x_s = mean + √variance · step_noise。

最後一跳 (s = 0) 不加 noise，直接回傳 mean。

獨有變數。沒有，它用到的東西全在共用表裡。

它連到誰。呼叫 ddpm_jump_posterior；效果上等於 sample_eta 在 eta = 1 (亂數流細節不同)；產生用於 evaluation 的樣本。

變形版 pipeline

make_anisotropic_diffusion_batch(batch_size, cfg, means, alpha_bars)

它在幹嘛。跟 make_diffusion_batch 一模一樣，只差乾淨樣本的來源換成 sample_anisotropic_gmm，也就是各 component 的 covariance 被拉長 (不是圓形) 的版本。

回傳值也少了 t_indices，只回 (x_t, t_model, noise)。

它連到誰。train_anisotropic_diffusion_model 裡面呼叫它。

train_anisotropic_diffusion_model(cfg, seed, alpha_bars, …)

它在幹嘛。跟 train_diffusion_model 相同的 loop，只把資料來源換成非等向的 batch 生產器。它的超參數 (training_steps、batch_size、learning_rate) 沒有預設值，必須明給。

它連到誰。產生非等向實驗用的 model (task 002/008 那條線)。

train_diffusion_model_on_dataset(train_data, cfg, seed, alpha_bars, …)

它在幹嘛。train_diffusion_model 的有限資料版。差別只有一行，它不是每步重新抽全新的 GMM 樣本，而是每批都從固定的 train_data 裡重抽。

這是研究「訓練資料有限時會發生什麼事」的工具。它只回傳 model。

獨有變數。

變數名裝什麼型態
train_data固定的有限訓練集tensor (N, dim)

它連到誰。有限資料實驗 (task 004/006 那條線)。
