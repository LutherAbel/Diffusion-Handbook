This page separates established theory, guaranteed mechanisms, completed empirical evidence, and open controls. The mode-separation crossover was tested with three seeds and did not persist after aggregation. That does not turn the result into a general method ranking.

1. Established facts: validate the implementation, do not rediscover the theory Supported
  • Linear Gaussian noising maps a GMM to another GMM. Component means and covariances have closed forms for the VP process.
  • Known mixture weights, means, and covariances give an analytic, responsibility-weighted GMM score. Use it as an oracle and test it against finite differences or autograd.
  • Population MSE targets a conditional expectation, under population-loss and sufficient-capacity assumptions.
  • Noise, score, and clean-sample parameterisations are algebraically convertible when schedule and normalisation are consistent.
  • Exact reverse dynamics and the probability-flow ODE recover the same continuous-time marginals under their regularity assumptions. This is not a finite-step learned-model ranking result.
  • DDIM can reuse the DDPM training objective. A consistent solver converges as step size tends to zero under smoothness and controlled-error assumptions.
2. Guaranteed mechanisms that do not determine a winner Supported

Changing covariance or mode separation changes the score or velocity field. Replacing a learned field with an exact one removes neural approximation error, but not prior mismatch, time truncation, discretisation, stochastic variation, or metric-estimation noise.

A nonzero DDPM noise term produces conditional sampling variance. This may help or hurt final SWD. Likewise, more NFE reduces nominal step size but does not guarantee a monotone finite-sample SWD curve.

1. 已建立的事實：驗證實作，不需要重新做成研究實驗 Supported
  • 線性的 Gaussian noising 會把 GMM 送到另一個 GMM。對 VP process，每個 component 的 mean 與 covariance 都有 closed form。
  • 已知 weights、means 與 covariances 時，GMM score 有 responsibility-weighted 解析式。用它做 oracle，並以 finite difference 或 autograd 測試。
  • Population MSE 的最佳解是 conditional expectation，但需要 population loss 與足夠函數容量。
  • noise、score 與 clean-sample parameterisation 在 schedule 和 normalisation 一致時可互相轉換。
  • exact reverse dynamics 與 probability-flow ODE 在其 regularity assumptions 下可恢復相同的 continuous-time marginals。這不是 finite-step learned-model 的排名定理。
  • DDIM 可沿用 DDPM training objective。一致的 solver 在 smoothness 和受控 error 下會隨 step size 趨近零而收斂。
2. 必定正確的機制，但不能推出誰會贏 Supported

改 covariance 或 mode separation 會改變 score 或 velocity field。用 exact field 取代 learned field 會移除 neural approximation error，但 prior mismatch、time truncation、discretisation、stochastic variation 與 metric-estimation noise 仍可存在。

非零 DDPM noise term 會產生 conditional sampling variance。它可能改善或變差最終 SWD。更多 NFE 會降低 nominal step size，但不保證 finite-sample SWD curve 單調。
